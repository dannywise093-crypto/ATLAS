# NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES — Brand Identity

## Parent organization
**NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES**

Corporate descriptor: **Next-Generation Engineering, Research & Advanced Artificial Intelligence.**

Corporate motto: **Building Intelligence. Engineering Tomorrow.**

## Flagship product
**NEXORA AI** — the flagship artificial-intelligence platform for engineering, scientific research, software, robotics, and technology development.

Product promise: **Beyond Intelligence. Engineering the Future.**

## Product family
- NEXORA AI — flagship intelligence platform.
- NEXORA ENGINEERING — engineering design and analysis.
- NEXORA RESEARCH — scientific research and knowledge workflows.
- NEXORA ROBOTICS — robotics discovery, simulation, and safe telemetry.
- NEXORA LABS — experimentation and innovation.
- NEXORA SYSTEMS — software and infrastructure.

## Visual system
- Primary background: midnight navy (`#07101f`).
- Primary accent: electric blue (`#168bff`).
- Secondary accent: silver (`#aebed2`).
- Typography: modern, legible sans-serif.
- Logo: geometric N monogram with silver framing and electric-blue strokes.

## Naming rules
- Use **NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES** for the parent organization.
- Use **NEXORA AI** for the main application and flagship product.
- Use **NEXORA** as the short visual brand where context is clear.
- Use the product-family names above for specialist divisions.
- Do not rename internal API identifiers, storage keys, worker bindings, or credentials solely for visual consistency. Migrate those separately with compatibility tests.

## Legal note
The name and identity are selected as a proposed brand direction. Trademark clearance, company registration, domain availability, and app-store name availability must be checked before public commercial launch.
